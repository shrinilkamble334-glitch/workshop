From assessment to switch-on

A straightforward sequence, usually six to ten weeks from first visit to live power.

1

Site assessment

We measure sun exposure, wind data or water flow on-site, then model expected output for your specific location.

2

Design & permits

A system is sized to your usage, drawings are filed, and utility interconnection paperwork is handled for you.

3

Install & monitor

Certified crews install and commission the system, with live output monitoring available from day one.
